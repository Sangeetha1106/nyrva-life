1. Introduction & Brand Philosophy

NYRVA LIFE ("we", "our", or "the brand") is committed to maintaining the privacy and trust of our customers. This Privacy Policy outlines the general practices governing the collection, use, and safeguarding of customer information across our website.

2. Information Collection

When you visit our website, register an account, or initiate an order inquiry, certain details may be collected to facilitate fulfillment:

  • Contact Details: Full name, telephone/WhatsApp number, and email address.
  • Delivery Address: Physical shipping address, city, state, and PIN code for order dispatch.
  • Order Records: Products selected (Men's Wellness Powder or Men's Wellness Capsules) and order preferences.
  • Payment Information: Handled entirely through encrypted third-party payment gateways; NYRVA LIFE does not store credit card numbers or CVV codes.

3. How Information is Utilized

Customer details are utilized strictly for order processing, logistics coordination, and direct customer care communications related to NYRVA LIFE herbal wellness purchases. We do not sell, rent, or trade customer contact details to third-party commercial marketing entities.

4. Data Security & Encryption

Our website employs industry-standard 256-bit SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) encryption to protect information in transit. Administrative access to order records is restricted to authorized operations personnel.

5. Policy Updates & Contact Information

NYRVA LIFE reserves the right to update this policy in accordance with legal and operational developments. Any revisions will be published directly on this page with an updated effective date.
