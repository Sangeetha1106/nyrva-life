1. Order Cancellations

Customers may request an order cancellation prior to product dispatch by reaching out to our customer support desk via phone or WhatsApp at 8667698604. Once a package has been handed over to our courier partner for transit, standard return guidelines apply.

2. Hygienic Safety & Return Eligibility

Because NYRVA LIFE supplies ingestible wellness consumables (herbal powder containers and wellness capsules), strict hygiene standards apply:

  • Products must be in their original, unopened condition with outer security seal intact.
  • Containers that have been unsealed, opened, or partially consumed cannot be returned due to health and safety regulations.
  • Items received damaged, leaking, or with broken exterior seals during transit must be reported to our care desk within 24 to 48 hours of delivery.

3. Damaged or Incorrect Shipments

In the unlikely event that you receive a damaged container or an incorrect product format, please contact our support desk immediately with clear unboxing photographs or video. Upon verification, NYRVA LIFE will arrange a prompt replacement shipment at no additional cost.

4. Refund Processing

For approved returns or cancellations, refunds will be credited back to the original source payment method (UPI, Debit/Credit Card, or Net Banking) or via verified digital transfer for Cash on Delivery orders, in accordance with payment gateway settlement timelines (typically 5 to 7 business days following inspection).

5. How to Initiate a Return Request

To initiate a return or replacement inquiry:

  • Contact our customer support helpline or message us on WhatsApp at 8667698604.
  • Provide your contact details, product name (Powder or Capsules), and brief description of the issue.
  • Our support representative will guide you through the return authorization steps.
